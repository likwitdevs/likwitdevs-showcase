## Short summary

Likwit Blvd is an artist-run portfolio and storefront for airbrush artwork, tattoo work, custom commissions, and original products.

## Problem solved

Social profiles can show individual pieces of work, but they do not provide a durable way to organize a growing portfolio, explain commission options, or build a recognizable creative brand. Likwit Blvd creates a central destination for artwork, inquiries, and future product sales.

## Features built

- Visual portfolio for airbrush and tattoo work
- Branded presentation for original artwork
- Commission and custom-work inquiry paths
- Mobile-friendly gallery experience
- Product and collection foundations
- Flexible structure for future storefront growth
- Clear connection between the artist’s work and brand identity

## Public-safe tech stack

- Responsive portfolio and storefront interface
- Media-focused gallery presentation
- E-commerce-ready application structure
- Secure inquiry integrations
- Managed production hosting
- Search and social-sharing metadata

Commerce configuration, content-management tools, and operational implementation remain private.

## Business value

Likwit Blvd gives the creative brand an owned destination beyond social media. It makes the portfolio easier to browse, gives potential customers a direct path to discuss custom work, and establishes a foundation for future product sales.
