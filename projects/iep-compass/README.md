## Short summary

IEP Compass is an AI-assisted education product that helps parents understand their child’s Individualized Education Program and prepare more focused questions for school meetings.

## Problem solved

IEP documents are often long, technical, and difficult to review under pressure. Important services, goals, accommodations, and follow-up questions can be buried in specialized education language. IEP Compass gives families a clearer, more structured way to prepare before a meeting without presenting itself as a substitute for professional, educational, or legal guidance.

## Features built

- Document upload and pasted-text review
- Plain-language meeting-preparation reports
- Structured coverage of services, goals, concerns, questions, and next steps
- Multilingual support
- Downloadable report output
- Responsive experience for desktop and mobile use
- Privacy-conscious document processing

## Public-safe tech stack

## Business value

IEP Compass turns a stressful document-review task into a more approachable preparation workflow. It also demonstrates Likwit Devs’ ability to design and build a focused software product that combines document handling, AI-assisted analysis, privacy considerations, and a clear user experience.
