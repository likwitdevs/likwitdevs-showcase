## Short summary

This case study documents Likwit Devs’ contribution to the NW AutoFix public website rebuild, with work focused on customer trust, mobile usability, service visibility, and local search structure.

## Problem solved

The previous website had SSL and reliability problems that could prevent customers from reaching the business confidently. Service information also needed clearer organization so vehicle owners could quickly understand what the shop handles and how to get help.

## Features built

- Secure, reliable public website experience
- Organized service pages for common repair needs
- Mobile-first navigation and readable service content
- Trust-focused presentation for local customers
- Clear call and contact pathways
- Local search structure and metadata
- Improved technical foundation for ongoing updates

## Public-safe tech stack

- Next.js website
- Responsive component-based interface
- Local SEO metadata
- Performance-focused frontend delivery
- Secure HTTPS hosting
- Contact and lead-generation pathways

Hosting credentials, form routing, analytics, and implementation details remain private.

## Business value

The public-facing rebuild replaced an unreliable customer touchpoint with a secure, professional site that works across devices. Customers can review services and contact the shop with less friction, while the business has a stronger foundation for local search visibility.
